# Commercial use

This project is free for noncommercial use. Any commercial use needs a paid
commercial licence.

## What is free

Free use means use for a permitted purpose under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE):

- personal use — your own projects, your own machine, your own data;
- research, study and teaching, including at a university or a school;
- hobby projects and experiments that are not for money;
- open work for a charity, a non-profit or a community group;
- a public body: a government institution, a public research organisation, a
  public health or public safety organisation, or an environmental protection
  organisation.

Using the software inside a company, a paid product or a paid service is not
free, even when the software itself costs you nothing.

## What needs a paid licence

Commercial use needs a licence from the copyright holder. That includes:

- using the software in a business or for a business, including internal use
  inside a company;
- shipping it inside a paid product or a paid service;
- running it as part of something you charge money for.

## How to ask

- Open an issue in this repository titled "Commercial licence request" and
  describe what you want to use it for; or
- contact the owner through https://github.com/Diclean.

You will get a price and the terms. Until a commercial licence is signed, the
noncommercial terms apply.
